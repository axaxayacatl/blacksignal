RESEARCH PROTOCOL

METHOD NOTES // 009

HISTORICAL DOCUMENTATION: Records should distinguish surviving historical evidence from later interpretations, folklore, and contemporary reconstructions.

ICONOGRAPHIC ANALYSIS: Visual representations are examined in relation to their cultural, religious, and historical contexts.

DEMONOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION: Spirit names, hierarchies, and attributes are catalogued according to the specific texts and traditions in which they appear. Different sources may disagree.

SOURCE INTEGRITY: A historical claim should be traceable to a publication, manuscript, archaeological record, or credible research institution. Unverified interpretations should remain identified as such.

RESEARCH STATUS: HISTORICALLY DOCUMENTED indicates evidence supported by historical or archaeological records. FRAGMENTARY indicates incomplete or disputed evidence. INTERPRETIVE indicates modern artistic, symbolic, or analytical interpretation.
